# BetterLal-lo source ledger

This is a non-published research ledger. It records where a claim came from and what the source can support. The app only receives a claim after a human reviewer confirms the jurisdiction, source period, value, and wording.

## Source rules

- A direct official page or document is required for a published factual claim.
- The authority column describes the source publisher, while the jurisdiction column describes the record’s geographic scope.
- Historical and preliminary records are allowed when the period or qualification remains visible in the app.
- A general agency homepage is a lead, not evidence for a Lal-lo-specific fee, requirement, contact, or project.
- Search results, third-party directories, and social posts are leads only.
- Conflicting official records remain pending until the conflict is reconciled.
