# Budget and financial records

The financial dashboard publishes verified annual Statement of Receipts and Expenditures snapshots from the Bureau of Local Government Finance (BLGF) for FY2023-FY2025. These are reported financial records from the BLGF LIFT system, not an audit opinion and not an approved-budget statement.

- [BLGF local finance data](https://blgf.gov.ph/lgu-fiscal-data/)
- [BLGF FY2023 SRE workbook](https://blgf.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/By-LGU-SRE-2023.xlsx)
- [BLGF FY2024 SRE workbook](https://blgf.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/By-LGU-SRE-2024.xlsx)
- [BLGF FY2025 SRE workbook](https://blgf.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/By-LGU-SRE-2025.xlsx)

Approved-budget information remains a separate source track. The [DBM FY2026 table set](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/index.php/2026/budget-of-expenditures-and-sources-of-financing-fy-2026) links F.7, F.8, and F.9 municipality statements for FY2024-FY2026. BetterLal-lo does not reproduce a DBM amount until the exact Lal-lo row, fiscal year, issuing office, and revision status are checked and approved.

## Where to look

- Municipal budget and expenditure publications from the Municipality of Lal-lo
- [Commission on Audit](https://www.coa.gov.ph/) audit reports and financial records
- [Department of Budget and Management Open Data](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/index.php/open-data)
- [DBM F.7 — municipalities, FY 2024](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/BESF/BESF2026/F7.pdf)
- [DBM F.8 — municipalities, FY 2025](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/BESF/BESF2026/F8.pdf)
- [DBM F.9 — municipalities, FY 2026](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/BESF/BESF2026/F9.pdf)
- [DILG Full Disclosure Policy Portal reports](https://fdpp.dilg.gov.ph/fdpp/report)
- The municipality’s transparency-seal or public-information postings, if available

## What to check

When reviewing a budget record, note the fiscal year, document title, approving body, date published, and whether it is a proposed, approved, amended, or year-end report.

Source policy: link the original government document wherever possible.

Quarterly Lal-lo SRE values and individual Full Disclosure Policy documents remain unpublished until their exact portal records are matched and reviewed.

The [COA Lal-lo Water District compliance audit](https://www.coa.gov.ph/wpfd_file/lal-lo-water-district-cagayan-compliance-audit-report-2024/) is a separate water-district record and is not used as a municipal-government audit.
