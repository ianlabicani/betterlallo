# Budget and financial records

Budget information should be read from an official publication for the correct fiscal year. BetterLal-lo does not reproduce amounts until the underlying document, year, issuing office, and revision status have been verified.

## Where to look

- Municipal budget and expenditure publications from the Municipality of Lal-lo
- [Commission on Audit](https://www.coa.gov.ph/) audit reports and financial records
- [Department of Budget and Management Open Data](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/index.php/open-data)
- The municipality’s transparency-seal or public-information postings, if available

## What to check

When reviewing a budget record, note the fiscal year, document title, approving body, date published, and whether it is a proposed, approved, amended, or year-end report.
