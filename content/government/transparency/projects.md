# Projects and procurement

Project information is most useful when residents can connect the project name to its location, budget, implementing office, contractor or partner, status, and official procurement record.

## Where to look

- Municipal project announcements and accomplishment reports
- Official procurement notices and Philippine Government Electronic Procurement System records
- Cagayan provincial and municipal bids-and-awards publications
- COA observations and audit reports where relevant

The portal now includes dated examples from the [DPWH Region II FY2024 program](https://www.dpwh.gov.ph/dpwh/sites/default/files/gaa2024region02.pdf), the [Cagayan provincial share-of-LGU report](https://cagayan.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Share-of-LGU.pdf), and [DILG Region II project reporting](https://www.region2.dilg.gov.ph/index.php/news). Each record keeps the owning agency visible and preserves program-listing or reported-status language; none is silently converted into a municipal completion claim.

For current notices, use the [PhilGEPS portal](https://www.philgeps.gov.ph/) and the municipality’s official publications. A PhilGEPS notice is evidence of a published procurement notice, not proof of award or delivery.
