# Ordinances and resolutions

Municipal ordinances and resolutions should be read from an official copy issued by the Sangguniang Bayan, Municipal Secretary, or another authorized government source.

When a document becomes available, record its number, title, approval date, issuing body, source link, and whether a later measure amended or repealed it.

For a document that is not online, request a copy from the Office of the Sangguniang Bayan or the Municipal Secretary. BetterLal-lo does not treat an unverified social-media image or repost as the definitive record.

Reference: [Official Gazette — Republic Act No. 7160](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1991/10/10/republic-act-no-7160/)

Checked: 21 September 2026
