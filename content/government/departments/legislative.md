# Legislative Department

The **Sangguniang Bayan** is the municipal legislative body of Lal-lo. It considers local ordinances and resolutions and performs the legislative functions assigned to municipalities under Philippine local-government law.

## Finding ordinances and resolutions

This portal will link to copies of Lal-lo ordinances and resolutions once they are published by an official municipal, provincial, or national government source. Until then, residents should request the current record from the Office of the Sangguniang Bayan or the Municipal Secretary.

When reviewing a document, check its title, number, approval date, issuing office, and whether a later measure amended or repealed it.
