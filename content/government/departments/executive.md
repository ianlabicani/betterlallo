# Executive Department

The executive branch of the **Municipality of Lal-lo** is led by the Municipal Mayor and includes the municipal offices that deliver local programs and services.

## Municipal Mayor

The Provincial Government of Cagayan’s municipal directory abbreviates the mayor’s name as **Oliver Pascual**. A newer provincial publication dated 8 May 2026 spells the name as **Hon. Florence Oliver Pascual**. BetterLal-lo displays the fuller name while keeping both official source contexts visible for future reconciliation.

For official announcements, appointments, signatures, and current office contact details, verify directly with the Municipal Hall or the municipality’s official information channels before relying on them.

## Municipal offices

The municipal executive offices typically coordinate local administration, budgeting, planning, health, social welfare, agriculture, engineering, environment, disaster risk reduction, and public safety. This portal will add individual office contacts as each one is verified from an official publication.
