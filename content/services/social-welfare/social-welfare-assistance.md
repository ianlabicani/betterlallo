# Find Social Welfare Assistance

The right assistance channel depends on the person’s situation and the current program call. Start with the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** or your barangay and ask for a current assessment.

## Ask about

- assistance for older persons, solo parents, and persons with disabilities;
- family, child, and community welfare support;
- emergency or disaster assistance; and
- livelihood, referral, or skills-training programs.

Ask which documents are required, whether an interview or social-worker assessment is needed, where the application is received, and how the decision or release will be communicated. Do not submit original identity documents to an unofficial intermediary.

For national program information, use the [Department of Social Welfare and Development](https://www.dswd.gov.ph/) website and its current regional contacts.
