# Report an Infrastructure Concern

Use the barangay as the first receiving point for a local road, bridge, drainage, water, streetlight, or public-facility concern unless an active emergency requires the disaster-response channel.

## Include these details

- exact location and nearest landmark;
- the type of problem and when it began;
- photographs or video when safe;
- whether people, vehicles, homes, or utilities are at immediate risk; and
- your name and a way to receive a response, if you choose to provide them.

Ask the receiving office for a reference number or acknowledgement and whether the concern was forwarded to the Municipal Engineering Office, planning office, water provider, barangay, or another agency. This portal does not accept reports or guarantee a project response.

## Official reference

- [Department of Public Works and Highways](https://www.dpwh.gov.ph/) — national public-works information.
- [Philippine Government Electronic Procurement System](https://www.philgeps.gov.ph/) — public procurement notices and records.
- [BetterLal-lo public information sources](../../government/overview/data-sources) — verification policy for local project information.
