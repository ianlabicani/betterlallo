# Find Agriculture and Fisheries Support

Residents, farmers, and fisherfolk can use this guide to identify the right office for agricultural inputs, livestock concerns, fisheries support, training, and livelihood assistance in Lal-lo.

## Start here

1. Describe your request clearly: crop or livestock type, barangay, season, and the support needed.
2. Ask the **Municipal Agriculture Office** or the appropriate barangay office which program is currently open.
3. Bring a valid ID and proof of residence or farm/fishing activity only when the office confirms that these are required.
4. Ask for the current application form, deadline, eligibility rules, and release schedule. Programs and requirements vary by funding cycle.

Possible support may include seeds or planting materials, livestock or veterinary services, fisheries assistance, farm tools, training, and referrals to provincial or national programs. This portal does not treat a general program description as proof that a specific allocation is currently available.
