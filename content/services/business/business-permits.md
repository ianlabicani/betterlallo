# Start or Renew a Business Permit

Business permit requirements depend on the business activity, location, ownership, and the current municipal process. Use this checklist to prepare questions for the **Municipal Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)** and your barangay.

## Before applying

- Confirm the business name and location, including the barangay where it will operate.
- Ask the barangay about a current barangay clearance requirement.
- Ask the municipal BPLO for the latest application form, documentary requirements, fees, assessment steps, and filing deadline.
- Confirm whether renewals, inspections, payments, or appointments are handled through a current online or in-person process.
- Check national registrations that may apply, including [DTI business-name registration](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/), [SEC registration](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/), and [BIR registration](https://www.bir.gov.ph/).

## Bring only what is confirmed

Requirements can change by business type. Ask the BPLO for the current list before preparing copies. Commonly requested documents may include identification, proof of address or lease, registration papers, and previous permit records, but this list is not a substitute for the municipal checklist.

Do not pay through an unofficial intermediary. Request an official assessment or receipt for every payment.
