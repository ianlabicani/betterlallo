# Emergency Information and Disaster Preparedness

For immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency responder first. Do not wait for this portal to load or rely on an old number if an official responder, barangay, or local emergency channel provides a current instruction.

## Verified Cagayan reference

The [Cagayan Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (PDRRMO)](https://pdrrmo.cagayan.gov.ph/) publishes provincial disaster information. Its [We Care Lal-lo program page](https://pdrrmo.cagayan.gov.ph/we-care-lal-lo-program-municipality-of-lal-lo/) identifies the Lal-lo disaster-response program and lists **0927-181-9424** as a hotline. The PDRRMO page describes the number as available for text or call; confirm current availability when possible.

The [PDRRMO contact page](https://pdrrmo.cagayan.gov.ph/elementor-1303/) lists **pdrrmo@cagayan.gov.ph** and **0975-434-8083**. The [Cagayan MDRRM directory](https://pdrrmo.cagayan.gov.ph/cagayan-mdrrmos/) should be checked for the latest local responder details.

## Prepare before an emergency

- Save the current PDRRMO and local responder contacts on your phone.
- Follow official weather, flood, earthquake, volcanic, and evacuation advisories.
- Keep medicines, water, food, light, identification, and essential documents ready.
- Ask your barangay where the current evacuation area and warning channels are located.
- Report your location, the hazard, injuries, and the number of people affected as clearly as possible.
