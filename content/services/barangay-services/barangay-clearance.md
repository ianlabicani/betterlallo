# Barangay Clearance

Barangay clearance requirements and fees can differ by purpose and barangay. Confirm the issuing barangay, current application form, identity or residency documents, fee, payment method, and release time directly with the barangay before preparing copies or paying.

## Prepare questions

- Explain the purpose of the clearance and ask whether another document is required as well.
- Ask which identification or proof of address is accepted.
- Request an official assessment and receipt for any payment.
- Confirm whether the clearance must be claimed in person or may be released through an authorized representative.
