# Find Environmental Programs and Reporting Channels

Environmental programs and enforcement channels depend on the concern and the responsible office. Start with the barangay or municipal environment office, then ask which agency has jurisdiction.

## For a local concern

Record the location, date, photographs where safe, and a short description. Ask for a receiving office, reference number, and expected next step. Do not enter a hazardous site or confront a person involved in a suspected violation.

Possible concerns include illegal dumping, open burning, quarrying, logging, water pollution, and damage to protected or agricultural areas. The responsible office may be municipal, provincial, or national depending on the concern.

## Official reference

- [DENR Regional Office 02](https://r2.denr.gov.ph/) — regional environmental programs and contact information.
- [Republic Act No. 9003](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/2001/01/26/republic-act-no-9003/) — national solid-waste-management framework.
