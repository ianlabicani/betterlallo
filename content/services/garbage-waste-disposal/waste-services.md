# Waste Collection, Segregation, and Reporting

Collection schedules and special pickup arrangements may differ by barangay. Ask your barangay or the municipal environment office for the current schedule and receiving channel before leaving waste outside a collection window.

## Practical checklist

1. Separate waste at source and follow the instructions currently issued by your barangay.
2. Keep recyclables, residual waste, biodegradable waste, and special or hazardous waste separate where the local schedule provides separate handling.
3. Ask where e-waste, batteries, medical waste, and other special waste may be accepted. Do not mix them with ordinary household waste.
4. For missed collection, illegal dumping, or open burning, record the location and time and report it to the barangay or the municipal environment office.

Republic Act No. 9003 is the national framework for ecological solid waste management. Read the [Official Gazette copy of RA 9003](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/2001/01/26/republic-act-no-9003/) alongside current local instructions.
