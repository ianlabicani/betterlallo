# Find Education Support and Scholarships

Education assistance changes by school year, funding source, and program call. Use this guide to find the current announcement instead of relying on an old deadline or a recycled social-media post.

## Where to verify

- Ask the school registrar, guidance office, or principal about school-based assistance and current requirements.
- Ask the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office**, the municipal education focal office, or your barangay whether an LGU-supported program is open.
- Check the [Department of Education](https://www.deped.gov.ph/) and [DepEd Cagayan Valley](https://region2.deped.gov.ph/) for official education announcements.
- Check the [Department of Science and Technology scholarship program](https://science-scholarships.ph/) and other national programs only through their official application instructions.

## Before submitting

Ask for the current eligibility rules, deadline, application form, proof requirements, and release method. Keep a copy or photo of the submitted form and any acknowledgement. Never pay a person who promises a scholarship slot.
