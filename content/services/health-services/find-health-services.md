# Find Health Services and Referrals

For a life-threatening emergency, use the current emergency-response channel or go to the nearest emergency facility. For routine care, start with the local health office or barangay health station and ask what service is available today.

## Ask about

- consultations, basic medicines, and referrals;
- maternal, newborn, and child health;
- routine immunization and nutrition services;
- animal-bite, tuberculosis, dengue, and other public-health programs; and
- the correct facility for laboratory tests, admission, or specialist care.

Bring identification and health records if available, but confirm document requirements before travelling. Service hours, medicine availability, and referral procedures can change.

## Official reference

- [Department of Health](https://doh.gov.ph/) — national health information and programs.
- [PhilHealth](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/) — membership and benefit information.
