# Municipal and Real Property Tax Payments

Municipal taxes and real property tax assessments should be confirmed with the appropriate Lal-lo office before payment. Current assessment rules, rates, penalties, due dates, documentary requirements, office hours, payment channels, and online links are not published here without a current official source.

## Before paying

- Identify whether the request concerns a business tax, real property tax, community tax, or another local assessment.
- Bring the previous assessment, tax declaration, official receipt, or account reference only when the office confirms it is needed.
- Ask for the current assessment and an official receipt for every payment.
- Do not send payment to a personal account or an unofficial intermediary.
